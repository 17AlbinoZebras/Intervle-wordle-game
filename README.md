## Intervle can be played at https://intervle.fun/

### Guess the word in 8 or fewer tries!
- <b>Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word.</b>
- <b>The colors represent how far each letter is from the one in the target word in that position.</b>
- <b>Distances are based on the alphabet and can go in either direction.</b>
- <b>The alphabet loops.
    - E.g. Both "G" or "Y" could be indicated as 4 letters away from "C"</b>
